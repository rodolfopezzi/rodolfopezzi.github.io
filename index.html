About

I am a PhD candidate in Sociology at Trinity College Dublin, supervised by Dr. Yekaterina Chzhen. My research sits at the intersection of social stratification, sociology of education, and life course inequalities, with a focus on how social origin shapes educational pathways from childhood to early adulthood.

My doctoral research focuses on how educational inequalities emerge and develop across the life course. Using longitudinal panel data from the National Educational Panel Study (NEPS), Growing Up in Ireland (GUI), and the COVID Social Mobility and Opportunities (COSMO) study, I analyse how differences in family resources shape educational trajectories across institutional contexts.

In the academic year 2025–2026 I also served as Lecturer in Social Inequality and Intergroup Relations at Dublin City University. Between September and December 2025 I was a visiting researcher at the DemoSoc Group, Universitat Pompeu Fabra, Barcelona, hosted by Dr. Luis Ortiz-Gervasi.
